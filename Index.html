AIT Hackthon Contest 2026

Join us for a high-energy, 24-hour innovation marathon where the brightest minds converge to build solutions for tomorrow's challenges. Whether you are a developer, designer, or hardware enthusiast, this is your stage to turn ideas into reality.

Key Milestones in Our Journey

2018: The Spark – Seven students build a life-saving app with no electricity, proving that innovation isn't about the tools, but the will to solve problems.

2021: The Virtual Shift – During the global lockdown, the event went digital, attracting over 2,000 developers from 15 different countries, breaking all previous participation records.

2024: The Hardware Revolution – Student-built rovers mapped campus tunnels during our first Robotics Show.

2026: The AI Frontier – Celebrating our 8th anniversary by merging human creativity with Neural Networks to redefine coding.
